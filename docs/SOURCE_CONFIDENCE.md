# Source Confidence Rules

Confidence measures evidence quality and completeness, not model certainty.

## Tiers

- Tier 1: official school, conference, NCAA, NFL, or combine records.
- Tier 2: structured aggregators with stable identifiers and reproducible data.
- Tier 3: consensus projections, reviewed scouting reports, and market sources.
- Tier 4: unverified reports, social posts, or unattributed claims. Tier 4 data cannot create a production grade by itself.

## Resolution rules

1. Prefer the freshest Tier 1 record for current roster and measurements.
2. Preserve conflicting observations and record the selected resolution.
3. Do not average incompatible measurements without documenting the method.
4. Declaration status must include evidence and an as-of date.
5. Missing inputs remain null and reduce confidence.
6. AI summaries must cite observation IDs and clearly distinguish fact from inference.

## Publication gate

A prospect may appear on the board with incomplete data, but an overall grade requires evidence-backed production plus at least one draft-projection or athletic/measurement component. Low-confidence grades must be labeled and excluded from strong automated recommendations.
